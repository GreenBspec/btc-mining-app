📖 About / How It Works

This is a Demo Mode Bitcoin mining simulator.

  • ⛏️ Mining is simulated locally in your browser.
  • 💰 BTC balances shown in the app are simulated balances.
  • 💸 Withdrawals are simulated and do not send real Bitcoin.
  • 👤 Accounts and demo activity are stored locally on the device.
  • 🔧 The Admin Dashboard is for managing the simulator.
  • 📱 The dashboard is designed for mobile devices.

Important: This application does not perform real Bitcoin mining, does not generate real BTC, and does not transfer real cryptocurrency.
